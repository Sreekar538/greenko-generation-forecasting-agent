    Limitations to remember

    • The supplied dataset is small and described as dummy data in the assignment.
    • It has two records per date but no plant or asset identifier.
    • It does not contain separate forecast-weather and observed-weather values.
    • Rule-based indicators do not prove the cause of a generation variance.
    • This demo does not call an external LLM and does not implement RAG.
